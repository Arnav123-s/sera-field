## Primary-source checks and corrections

[Hamiltonian Echo Backpropagation](https://arxiv.org/html/2103.04992v2) requires
Hamiltonian, time-reversible processing, controllable reversal and a separate
decay operation. Its error signal is a small perturbation: the gradient relation
is a linear-response result. The current dissipative settling cannot be relabeled
HEB. A proposed reversible bulk must be tested with the actual capture coupling;
calling the coupling orthogonal is not a reversibility proof.

[Generalising E-prop to Deep Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24506) is a
separate learning-method reference. It does not by citation instantiate
eligibility traces over this sheaf. Local derivative recursions and broadcast
signals require a model-specific derivation and measured credit assignment.

[Measurement-Only Topological Quantum Computation](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.0279)
concerns anyon charge measurements and quantum computation. To use such operators
for SERA, the semantic encoding and independent outcome checks must also be
specified. Computing a knot invariant does not alone certify a statement about
a source passage or a physical experiment.

The document excludes particle physics/QFT and later invokes topological QFT.
I interpret its executable direction as quantum-information/topological operators
without requiring high-energy field dynamics. Old ERG work is retained as a
historical research branch. This avoids discarding the usable sheaf/Clifford work.

Topological charge stability has boundary/non-singularity conditions; finite
changes or zero crossings can invalidate them. A protected code also needs a
verified decoding relation to a knowledge claim. Gauge-equivalent descriptions
preserve physical observables; changing drag is a different model intervention.
