## Mathematical audit

1. **Graded geometry:** implemented genuine Cl(3,0) products and induced rotor
   transport. The supplied arbitrary skew 8x8 exponential is an SO(8) operation;
   it can mix scalar/vector/bivector grades and need not preserve the geometric
   product. It therefore requires this repair before being called a Clifford rotor.
2. **Sheaf dynamics:** implemented both restriction maps and their adjoints. An
   edge residual belongs to its edge frame; adding it directly to a different
   vertex frame does not establish local covariance. The action derivative is
   checked against autograd and a quadratic-form identity.
3. **Condensation:** implemented an explicit quartic radial potential and learned
   persistent prior inside the actual field. Its action flow is dissipative. The
   supplied tanh gate alone does not implement its stated free-energy dynamics.
4. **Imagination and facts:** read-only/out-of-place field evolution preserves
   parameters; independent evidence gates persistent learning. Gauge changes
   preserve physical observables, whereas changed mass is a changed physical
   condition. No unlimited-memory or gauge-alone protection claim is adopted.
5. **Topology and exact truth:** winding/charge stability needs a nonvanishing
   field, boundary conditions and an allowed-deformation class. Crossing a zero,
   boundary or singularity can change the charge. A charge additionally needs a
   verified decoding map to mean a particular proposition. The independent exact
   and empirical checkers remain active while that mapping is researched.
6. **ERG/growth:** the Wetterich trace flow does not itself specify a cell-attachment
   operator, a new cognitive variable or preservation of old learned outputs.
   Field content, regulator, truncation, projections and a growth map remain exact
   derivation tasks; a residual threshold is not renamed ERG.
7. **e-prop:** a learning derivative/eligibility trace is not generally identical
   to field curvature. This study retains the verified score-function bridge.
8. **Storage:** Clifford coefficients and sheaf maps can be represented using
   arrays while retaining geometric meaning. This is not a demonstration that
   numerical arrays prohibit geometry. Measure all state/weight/optimizer costs.
9. **Grassmannians/anyons:** no cognitive cell-growth algorithm or representation
   of a braid group was supplied by the snippet. Those claims stay separate from
   the implemented graph-sheaf core, with explicit derivation obligations.
