# Research and behavior audit after connected teaching

I re-read the original proposal after building the connected models and compared
it with expanded THDFT, SCFE and the latest Singular Ecosystem text. Original
bytes, older audits, models and unsuccessful results remain preserved. This audit
distinguishes implemented mechanisms, demonstrated learning and exact next gates.
It makes no verdict about the eventual limits of the project.

## Evidence boundary

SCFE-006 subsequently qualified the saved 768-attempt policy on 512 new systems:
17.39% lower error than the unchanged policy, with a positive paired bootstrap
interval. It independently replayed and was integrated without changing any
non-policy parameter. The original unsuccessful final-step policy remains saved.

The two original final cohorts remain frozen and their raw records replay exactly
in fresh interpreters. Association tests include 38 calculus, 256 conversation,
99 grammar, 48 Plato, 33 programming and 256 dictionary cases. These are
four-candidate source associations. Program association does not score synthesis.

Only three eligible original one-step questions occur in the sampled mathematics
cohort; the main 512-case metric includes explicitly teacher-forced continuations.
Descartes was taught but has no held-out partition under this split. These details
determine the scope of the scores. Model weights, source identities, negative
results and all supervised costs are retained.
