# SERA Field

**SERA — State-Space Engine for Reasoning and Adaptation.**

I am building a persistent learner that forms grounded situations, imagines
alternatives, investigates missing information and retains checked discoveries.
This independent laboratory starts its models and weights from scratch. Its
training objectives concern evidence, executable programs and observed
consequences. The earlier SERA project is preserved separately.

The latest **SEMANTIC-015** owner interprets human-written premises and proposed
statements through one learned situation field. After teaching on **471,139 human
sentence pairs**, it reached **59.20% accuracy on 4,096 reserved examples**, up from
**34.62%** at initialization. The same owner retains measured physical acquisition,
conditional imagination, earlier reading and executable task routes.
[Teaching, comparisons and independent qualification](../reports/SEMANTIC-015/REPORT.md).

Its retained **GROW-013** route learns a missing response structure from measurements,
checks it on separate observations and reuses the acquired relation for prediction,
inverse questions, counterfactuals, planning and directional explanations. Its
coupled field combines covariance sheaves, temporal echo learning, a stationary
boundary transformation and verified condensate memory.

On 256 new simulated systems, the learned extension reached forward MSE **0.00454**
and **255/256** correct directional answers. It reduced prediction error by **89.5%**
against the same owner's four-feature route and **74.5%** against its untrained
extension. All six retention/identity checks passed and final records replayed
exactly. [Complete results](../reports/GROW-013/REPORT.md).

## Engineering

Human-trained word encoders and observation encoders feed one finite
**Clifford/sheaf situation field**. Graded multivectors, constrained rotor
connections and transported discrepancies participate in its computations.
The retained ECHO-007 core was trained through a checked reversible-credit
operator. Learned readouts propose source evidence, arithmetic programs and
three possible physical response models.

For new observations, a learned precision network controls an update to those
response models. The acquired coefficients are then shared by prediction,
inverse calculation, counterfactual imagination and trajectory planning. A
trained word/character interface selects the question's variable; a trained
meaning head uses the acquired model and operating point to determine the
direction. An explicit sentence form renders that result.

The investigation policy selects an observation for the original goal. The
independent checker distinguishes the requested intervention from the one
actually performed, measures whether the answer improved, and binds learning
credit to the goal, decision, model, source and evidence. Imagined branches keep
their assumptions. Source receipts and old revisions remain available after
correction.

The latest qualified SEMANTIC-015 owner has **891,972 parameters** and
**3,607,872 bytes of persistent model tensors**. Its
[checkpoint manifest](../checkpoints/SEMANTIC-015/MANIFEST.json) pins the exact
weights and configuration. The preserved CONCEPT-011 task owner has 876,997
parameters. Full training states, optimizer/RNG state, failed candidates, source
manifests and cost ledgers are preserved separately. The
[mathematical account](../docs/CONCEPT_ENGINEERING.md) explains which mechanisms are
engineered and which mappings SERA learned.

## Run a task

From this repository on the existing Windows environment:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts/supervise.py --attempt my-device-001 -- -m sera_field.session_cli --session local/my-device new --input examples/concept-task.json
```

This returns predictions, inverse input, changed-condition consequences, ranked
plans, a grounded explanation and a proposed next measurement from the same
saved owner. [Resume it, ask a new question, or add an actual observation](../docs/CONCEPT_USAGE.md).

The numerical supervisor reserves one CPU thread and caps its process tree at
2 GiB. The completed acquisition/release cycle used **23.1 supervised minutes**,
including the failed candidates, tests and independent replays. Earlier training
has its own preserved ledgers.
