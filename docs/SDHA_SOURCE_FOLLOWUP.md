# Follow-up source distinctions for the unified memory study

The new addendum is being tested as a connected learned update in the same owner.
The following primary-source checks refine the mathematical contracts and the
next human teaching source; they do not report a new trained capability.

[Demirtas et al., version 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.03223v2) relate ensembles
of neural functions and field distributions. Finite width and correlated parameter
distributions can produce connected higher moments. Their paper distinguishes
initialization from learning dynamics and derives approximate actions under
specified expansions. A nonlinear term alone does not implement their full
correlator-to-action construction. That construction remains a separately tracked
requirement of the source proposal; the existing finite Gaussian action has its
own narrower contract. Literal quantum-field interpretation additionally needs
the relevant axioms, beyond a non-Gaussian numerical state.

[Saha, Agudo-Canalejo and Golestanian](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.07101) give a
conserved nonreciprocal mixture with parameter-dependent phase separation and
traveling waves. Their traveling regime can itself coarsen through defect removal.
This reinforces the HISTORY-016 decision to record spatial wavelength and task
retrieval separately: nonreciprocity is an experimentally assessed mechanism,
not an automatic guarantee of retained distinctions.

[Williams, Nangia and Bowman](https://aclanthology.org/N18-1101/) describe human
premises across ten written/spoken genres, crowdworker-authored hypotheses and
additional manual validation. The corpus supplies a useful next teaching and
cross-genre assessment source for contextual language. The official archive is
preserved from [the authors' project page](https://cims.nyu.edu/~sbowman/multinli/),
with its exact identity and intake costs. OANC and source-specific fiction terms
remain attributed; raw corpus text is kept locally. Model training, split
qualification and any field-noise regularization study require a fresh prospective
protocol after the current owner is assessed.
