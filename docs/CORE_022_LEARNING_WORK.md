# Learning-procedure selection through the continuing owner

This pre-curriculum engineering route uses the existing shared choice map. It
selects among explicitly supplied practice schedules at an equal number of
updates. The schedule descriptions are public; assessment targets are not part
of the decision input. Selecting a supplied schedule is distinguished from
inventing a new optimizer or discovering its curriculum.

Freeze the practice records, schedules, independent acquisition and retention
checks, source groups and update limit before proposal. Save the actual owner,
optimizer, pending decision and random states before any practice. Each completed
update records the lesson identity, actual loss and cost, then re-encodes the
continuing goal's history. A checkpoint resumes at the next uncompleted update.

Evaluate a single deterministic proposed graph per independent case before and
after practice. An exact polynomial or finite-input assessor determines success;
proposal likelihood is not used as the correctness score. Assessment groups and
input views must be disjoint from the current practice records. These are
training-time procedure assessments, not the course's sealed final cohort.

Procedure reward is acquisition accuracy gain minus any retention accuracy loss,
minus 0.001 per completed update, clipped to [-1, 1]. Reconstruct decision-time
eligibility from the saved pre-practice owner; bind it to that exact decision and
predictor. Apply credit with the continuing learner's **same optimizer**, after
the measured practice updates. Record the effect of this additional policy update
separately. Preserve the original goal and retry it after returning the report.

Finite tests establish selection, split checks, actual teaching, bound delayed
credit, mid-trial resume, exact replay, costs and return. Curriculum teaching
must subsequently assess whether the procedure improves acquisition of the next
capability against matched supplied controls. A working selector or additional
points does not establish that benefit. No CORE-022 course starts from the
engineering fixture's weights.
