## Engineering before scaling training

The current SituationOwner and CreditBridge are a tested starting implementation,
not a completed semantic learner. Complete these concrete integrations in this
clone, updating the coverage rows with source/code/test identities:

**A. Goal-bound situation and memory.** Connect raw input views to a retained
situation, observed versus imagined fields, reference/roles, assumptions,

byte/character offsets and real state carried between views. The initial encoder's

**B. Learned proposals and evidence choice.** Learn candidate construction or
parameterized programs/controls from taught examples and outcomes; record every
supplied primitive, schema and candidate family. A fixed menu or the old twelve
force vectors is not the requested general imagination. Train the choice and
proposal weights through the actual owner. Preserve multiple valid hypotheses,
ambiguity and distinct assumptions. Define independent equivalence/deduplication

hidden mechanism IDs or labels supplied in the prompt.

**C. Real credit and retention.** Bind decisions, predictors, goals, observations,
intervention receipts and qualified assessments to CreditBridge or a tested
successor. Capture eligibility before outcomes. Do not mutate the owner with a
supervised update between a recorded decision and its delayed credit; either
resolve first or derive an explicitly tested off-policy treatment. Persist every
optimizer and sampler state used by the shared owner. Retire obsolete pending
decisions explicitly. Add balanced replay and measured retention; compare a
frozen earlier checkpoint, never silently restore to hide forgetting. Expand
the durable revision design if needed for long-run scale, preserving exact
anti-replay semantics and cost/exposure history.

**D. Independent subject checkers.** Physical simulation uses independent numerical
integration or observations, altered controls and checks on whether interventions
occurred. Mathematics uses exact arithmetic/algebra or a scoped proof checker.

AST parsing alone is not execution or correctness. Untrusted downloaded programs
must not be executed with unrestricted filesystem/network access. Reading spans
can provide foundation supervision; they do not alone verify situation semantics.
Humanities need a rubric that permits multiple supported interpretations. Do not
award factual/semantic credit by asking the learner to agree with its own output.
If an independent human assessment is unavailable, preserve those proposals as
pending and continue other assessable work.

**E. Paper-specific research.** Address every P01-P19 coverage row. In particular,
specify and test the proposed bulk/boundary projection, any effective-action

Research primary sources and alternatives before coding each new mechanism.
Derive units/shapes, stability, gradients/estimators, invariances, cost and failure
cases. The current finite action descent and delayed score-function estimator are
named approximations. Do not rename them Wetterich flow, holographic duality,
Chern-number memory or e-prop. Try concrete, justified variants; retain negative
findings. If a paper claim lacks a derivation, document the exact missing mapping,
test the nearest well-defined hypothesis and keep the underlying objective active.
Independent proof/outcome checks stay in place while storage mechanisms are tested.
