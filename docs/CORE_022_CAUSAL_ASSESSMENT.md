# One acquired relationship: inference, intervention and explanation

I rechecked the latest empirical synthesis while the registered fresh foundation
course was running. Its requested five uses remain a joint behavioral obligation:
trajectory prediction, hidden-parameter inference, changed physical conditions,
constrained trajectory planning and grounded language explanation. The existing
`core_cross_use.py` control-grid query is an engineering check of shared-state
reuse. It does not close these richer tasks.

## What the current simulation actually teaches

The frozen foundation teacher in `native_data.py` uses

    a(F, v) = F/m - d*phi(v) + b,
    phi(v) = v  or  v*abs(v).

Here `d*phi(v)` and `b` have acceleration units. This matters when teaching or
grading a change in mass. For a scale factor c > 0, the following are different
interventions on the same initially compatible relationship:

| Held fixed when mass changes to c*m | Counterfactual acceleration |
|---|---|
| Acceleration coefficients d and b | F/(c*m) - d*phi(v) + b |
| Drag force coefficient k=m*d and offset force B=m*b | (F-k*phi(v)+B)/(c*m) |
| Drag force k=m*d and body acceleration b, such as a specified uniform field | (F-k*phi(v))/(c*m) + b |

The actual experimental contract must say which intervention was performed.
Changing the requested force alone does not establish that mass was changed.
Likewise, multiplying every acceleration by 1/c assumes all contributing forces
stay fixed; it is not valid for every interpretation of the current teacher.
These distinctions do not change the ongoing registered course or its labels.
They constrain the later whole-system curriculum and its independent assessor.

An inverse force search is useful for choosing an action. Inverse mass inference
has a different evidence requirement. If force is calibrated, mass is positive
and the other contributions are unchanged, two distinct forces at the same
velocity give m=(F2-F1)/(a2-a1). A neural predictor can estimate that contrast,
but the conversion is a supplied mechanical assumption if performed in code.
It must not be reported as a discovered law or a directly learned mass readout.

With an unknown actuator gain g, response instead contains g*command/m. Scaling
g and m together leaves this contribution unchanged. A new observation of the
same type need not identify either value separately. Calibration or a different
measurement can distinguish them. The supplied Lorentz-force example has the
analogous unresolved component parallel to the observed velocity. The assessment
must preserve compatible alternatives until distinguishing evidence is acquired.

## Research alternatives and the resulting engineering choice

[Pawlowski, Castro and Glocker](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.06485v2) separate
abduction of the observed instance's exogenous state, intervention and prediction.
Their deep structural causal models use specified causal mechanisms and flow or
variational inference. I adopt the requirement to preserve the same factual
instance across hypothetical branches. I have not imported their pretrained
models or counted their results as SERA capabilities.

[Ahuja et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/ahuja23a/ahuja23a.pdf) study
representation identifiability from interventions. Their guarantees have explicit
decoder, support and intervention assumptions; the polynomial setting and more
general extensions have different requirements. I will compare calibrated and
imperfect interventions and record which latent quantities are identified, rather
than infer unique physical coordinates from prediction fit alone.

[Nasr-Esfahany and Kiciman](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.09031v1) demonstrate that
observational fit and known causal structure can still leave counterfactuals
unidentified. Their positive result has monotonic scalar-noise assumptions; their
broader analysis motivates measuring disagreement among compatible mechanisms.
For SERA, a narrow branch cloud is therefore a model-confidence quantity, while
independent failed predictions measure model adequacy.

The proposed implementation direction uses the current owner's input maps,
observed field, conditional copies and common response/choice/program weights.
Role-bearing quantities must be learned through that owner. It must not call a
separately fitted inverse model or use the evaluator's hidden mass as its input.
An analytic contrast, a fitted auxiliary decoder and the learned shared-field
route can serve as clearly labeled comparisons. No comparison determines the
research goal merely by being more accurate.

## Whole-system acceptance requirements

For each episode, save the original question and commit all five predictions
before revealing independent outcomes. Query the exact same factual state and
weight identity across views. Keep single-observation and targeted-acquisition
conditions distinct. Preserve all compatible methods, conditions and unperformed
branches. Check the actual intervention, including actuator behavior, rather
than trusting its requested label.

After evidence contradicts a committed prediction, revise the same learner and
return to its saved question. Recheck every view from one revised state. Evaluate
earlier valid regimes separately from the revised one. A change in scenario must
not silently reinterpret old measurements as measurements of the new scenario.

Language scores must distinguish source-answer selection, a learned directional
claim and a generated grounded explanation. An explanation needs correctly bound
entities, units, assumptions, intervention and supporting evidence. A renderer
that inserts independently supplied conclusions does not assess the learner's
language understanding. One contribution reused five ways receives one discovery
credit, with the five views providing evidence of reuse.

These are prospective acceptance details. No reserved foundation source answer
or final prediction was opened in this review. Counts, intervention families,
reward contracts and whole-assessment executables must be frozen before the
relevant behavioral teaching or final invocation. Foundation and matched-control
training continue unchanged; the complete research remains active.
