# Acceptance criteria for the requested SERA behavior

This revision follows the user's clarification after FIELD-001. The objective is a persistent learner that constructs grounded situations, imagines alternatives, notices and investigates gaps, discovers useful relationships and improves those procedures through independently verified reward. Classical model scores remain diagnostic evidence. Winning a classical benchmark is not the product goal or a condition for pursuing this research direction.

FIELD-001 is retained as a completed component study. Its completion does not close any untested whole-system requirement. The original SERA, the independent laboratories, the new field checkpoints, the failed attempts and the opened final cohort stay preserved.

The [broad curriculum](CURRICULUM.md) includes physical simulations, mathematics, coding, language, other sciences, humanities and practical tasks. Physics is one learning track; each additional track needs actual teaching and its own evidence of acquired behavior.

## What counts as understanding in the experiments

Understanding is assessed through behavior that requires the learned situation to be useful: interpret a new description, retain who knows or observes what, distinguish different meanings, infer missing consequences, predict an intervention, revise an explanation when evidence disagrees, and transfer the acquired relationship to a new question. A correct answer to a taught numerical family is one piece of evidence, not a substitute for this full assessment.

Imagination must have a causal role in the answer: the learner generates distinguishable candidate situations or mechanisms, predicts their consequences, finds a discriminating observation, and uses the observed result to revise what it believes. Record which candidates the learner actually generated before revealing the answer. Separate novel-to-this-learner discoveries from claims of novelty to science.

Perspective-taking is assessed with grounded observer information, goals and constraints. For example, changing who saw an object can change what an agent knows without changing where the object is. A spatial rotation test alone does not test this. Human analogies guide the requested behavior; subjective feelings are not assigned from successful task scores.

## Required integrated cycle

1. **Understand the current task.** A newly trained input encoder and shared learned situation must carry the language and observations into the investigation. Exact grammar, variables, annotations or instructor interventions that are supplied must be identified.
2. **Notice a gap.** The learner identifies an unresolved prediction, disagreement or insufficiently grounded meaning in its current knowledge. Preserve the original goal and the evidence available at that moment.
3. **Generate alternatives.** A learned procedure constructs several distinguishable candidate completions, explanations or solution approaches. Supplied action bounds and operation primitives are disclosed; they are not counted as invented concepts.
4. **Imagine consequences.** Each candidate predicts observable consequences and relevant counterfactuals through the shared situation model. Keep alternatives, assumptions, provenance and failures available for comparison.
5. **Investigate.** The learner chooses what evidence would resolve a relevant uncertainty. It must check that a requested intervention actually happened. An invented outcome cannot serve as the verification of its own imagination.
6. **Verify and reward.** Independent outcomes or a valid proof checker determine credit. Reward qualifies a contribution; it never purchases truth or permissions.
7. **Actually learn from credit.** A recorded reward must reach an explicit update of the learned proposal, investigation or consolidation procedure. Record parameters and optimizer state before and after. Merely adding points to a report is insufficient.
8. **Retain and return.** Store the checked relationship with its scope, revisit the original task, explain the supported answer, and test useful reuse and retention after further learning.

## Reward contract

The desired incentive is broad, accurate exploration. Score an independently checked improvement first. Give additional credit for a genuinely different verified explanation, valid method, assumption regime, useful connection or counterexample that increases coverage of the original question. Record the distinctness criterion before evaluation.

Count aliases, repeated evidence and equivalent predictions once. Do not reward the raw number of imagined branches, confidence, page count or verbal elaboration. Novelty is relative to the learner's retained experience unless independent external evidence establishes a stronger claim. Model-conditional practice remains separate from observed factual learning.

Learning-to-learn credit requires an independent improvement in acquiring a subsequent ability, measured with a matched source, practice and resource budget. An increase in points or the addition of another stored fact is insufficient. Preserve all unsuccessful attempts and total acquisition costs.

## Behavior gates still open after FIELD-001

| Gate | Evidence required before marking complete |
|---|---|
| Learned language-to-situation grounding | Held-out human wording, reference, ambiguity and perspective tests; evidence the shared situation affects answers |
| Self-chosen investigation | Tasks in which the learner identifies the unresolved issue without being told the missing mechanism or correct question |
| Learned alternative generation | Candidate records committed before answers are revealed; several verified, distinguishable approaches; transfer beyond taught combinations |
| Reward-driven improvement | A durable policy/learner update caused by independent credit, plus improved behavior on untouched tasks at matched evidence cost |
| Persistent broad exploration | Continued investigation across varied abilities, useful completion of earlier open goals, measured retention and avoidance of repeated easy-task farming |
| Representation discovery | A learned decision to introduce or revise a useful representation; variables and candidate mechanisms supplied by engineering are separately recorded |
| Better learning procedures | Improved verified acquisition on new tasks; both acquisition and evaluation costs included |
| Semantic/philosophical interpretation | Human-authored source identity, alternative interpretations, supporting evidence, explicit ambiguity and independent assessment |

These are open engineering and evaluation requirements. Their status says what work remains; it does not assert that the intended capability is impossible.

## Next integrated work package

Replace the earlier assumption that adding a text encoder alone is the next sufficient milestone. Prepare one connected acquisition-and-imagination study with language grounding, learned proposal/investigation decisions, independently verified credit and actual persistent updates. The checkpointed owner must connect these operations before claiming the loop is implemented.

Before training, fix the initial knowledge, human-source identities, supplied annotations, permitted evidence interface, independently assessed task families, reward definition and untouched transfer/retention cohorts. Include reward-disconnected and imagination-disconnected checks to establish whether those components actually change later behavior. Such checks diagnose the requested mechanisms; leaderboard rank is not the acceptance criterion.

The THDFT-specific mathematical work remains explicit: derive a discretized action and boundary/bulk coupling; define any representation-growth operator; derive an eligibility update rather than naming curvature an eligibility trace; and distinguish stable storage from verified truth. An engineering approximation must be named as such. The full proposed substrate must not be declared implemented because a connection-weight network uses related notation.
