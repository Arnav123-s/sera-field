# Executable imagination and qualified portfolio: construction contract

I am adding this route before the CORE-022 course starts. No earlier final is
used for selection. Engineering fixtures may set output biases to exercise a
particular decision; those are not taught capabilities or discovery results.

## Alternatives and decision

[DeepCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01989v2) uses learned program properties to
guide search. [DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381v1) combines program
search with learned abstractions and a recognition model. [Conflict-driven
synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.08029) learns exclusions from failed search.
These motivate executable candidates, reusable findings and explicit failures.
They do not establish that this field has acquired those procedures.

I use a parallel finite graph proposal map on the **existing imagined field**.
All operation, operand and output scores are computed together; no pretrained
model or next-token objective supplies programs. Each sampled graph is an
acyclic scalar program, with at most four inputs and eight instructions. The
initial construction uses four instructions and supplied rational constants
-2, -1, 0, 1 and 2. Add, subtract, multiply, divide, negate, square, absolute
value, minimum and maximum are supplied executable primitives. Their invention
is not credited to the learner. This scope includes arithmetic and bounded
scalar coding; arbitrary-language synthesis needs its own representation.

The conditional fusion probabilities choose a branch. Its shared field features
produce structural choices. Only reachable instructions contribute to the
decision likelihood. Dead code, identity operations, commutative permutations,
associative regrouping, repeated negation and square versus repeated multiplication
are canonical aliases. Factored and distributed constructions remain separately
inspectable. No sampled program executes Python, files, network or shell access.

## Evidence and credit

Commit proposals, predictor, retained-state identity, source, assumptions and
original goal before checking. The independent assessor receives a sealed local
contract after proposal; its target never enters proposal features. Two contracts
remain distinct: exact rational polynomial identity, or exact execution on a
declared finite set of rational inputs. Passing the latter certifies those tests,
not an identity over all inputs. Division by zero and unsupported algebraic
claims are preserved as failures. The proof checker expands coefficients through
its own recursive implementation; it does not trust the proposal's execution.

An independently qualified first solution can earn progress credit. A different
exactly verified construction may earn a diminishing, bounded method bonus.
Finite-test alternatives earn further credit for new checked coverage or lower
execution cost. Aliases, repeated evidence, changed predictors and relabelled
goals cannot farm novelty. Retain valid alternatives even when additional credit
is zero. Keep failed candidates, assumptions and assessment cost.

The algebraic identity scope concerns the represented polynomial. The executable
arithmetic service separately rejects numerators or denominators exceeding 256
bits; that resource contract is not a counterexample to the polynomial identity.
Renamed assumption wording does not create a new exact identity or new finite
input/output evidence. Full reviews are stored once, with a compact review index.

Credit updates the **same AdamW optimizer and all connected owner weights**.
No separate solver is trained behind the interface. Checked evidence is then
encoded back into the same situation; hypothetical outputs alone are not facts.
The original question is answered with qualified programs, their assessment
scope and an engineered explanation of the execution. This rendering is not
reported as learned free-form explanation.

## Engineering acceptance, before curriculum

Check independent algebra against exact numeric execution, adversarial partial
functions, finite-test versus proof distinctions, graph validity and aliases.
Check actual gradients into the input, common energy, memory and fusion paths.
Check proposal purity, committed-proposal validation, duplicate/stale credit,
bounded novelty, a genuine update, rollback, saved alternatives and exact restart.
Check that a new goal retains prior contribution identities. Preserve earlier
owner specifications with no proposal head. Recheck whole-repository regression
and a separate-process next-update replay under the one-thread, 2 GiB supervisor.

The full curriculum and untouched evaluation will be frozen separately after
the remaining whole-core acceptance. Capability claims require that teaching and
assessment; engineering tests establish the route and its evidence contracts.
