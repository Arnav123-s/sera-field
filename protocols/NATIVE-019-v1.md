## Question and interpretation

Can one freshly initialized owner learn an observation-to-history-to-imagination
route, when its input encoder, geometric connections, active memory dynamics and
answer maps are trained together from the first lesson? Compare memory available
from update 1 with memory enabled halfway through an otherwise matched course.
This tests a specified learning schedule; it cannot retrospectively identify the
cause of HISTORY-016's step-zero selection.

## Arms and fixed curriculum

Seed 19119; identical initial tensors and teaching order in three fresh arms:

1. `native`: memory available throughout all 8,192 updates.
2. `delayed`: no observation history in answers during updates 1--4,096;
   the full jointly trainable history path is enabled for updates 4,097--8,192.
3. `empty`: queries receive the empty history throughout the course.

All arms receive the same 8,192 batches of 32. Cycle: semantic, physics, semantic,
human-pairs, semantic, reading, semantic, mathematics. This is 131,072 semantic
presentations and 32,768 episodes/presentations for each remaining track per arm.
Human-pairs cycle the existing dictionary, conversation, grammar, philosophy,
calculus and programming tracks. The supplied tasks and annotations are recorded.
Physical episodes have four observed measurements, then three withheld queries.
No next-token prediction objective is used.

Use AdamW, learning rate 0.001, weight decay 0.0001, gradient norm cap 1.0.
Architecture: 16,384-entry learned word table, width 48, eight vector stalks,
finite learned text-local encoding, three conditional branches, four boundary
settling steps and two active-bulk relaxation steps per observed update.
Bounded learned couplings are nonzero initially. Every 1,024 updates, evaluate
the same development records. Select by the arithmetic mean of semantic,
human-pair, reading and arithmetic accuracy plus 1/(1+physical MSE), divided by
five; break ties by earlier update. Save an exact revision at least every 256
updates. No outcome-dependent increase in teaching or hyperparameter search.

## Assessment, interventions and decision

After all training, register checkpoint hashes, source/group IDs and final
cohorts once. Assess each arm, its initial state, and the native selected owner
with history erased, bulk removed, equal-rate traces and imagination bypassed.
These are inference interventions, not separately trained algorithm rankings.
Report counts, accuracy, log loss, physics MSE, changed-input consequences,
control choice error on a fixed 17-force grid and error versus history length.
Replay the primary final in a fresh process. Preserve every case and failure.

Before training, verify nonzero first-lesson gradients into the observation
encoder, geometry, memory rates/flow/readout and answer map; direct finite
differences where practical. Verify gradients are not detached at a supplied
error vector. Verify no premise bypass, no annotation input, branch isolation,
observed/conditional state distinction and exact restart. Account for parameter,
buffer, state, optimizer and process storage separately.

A useful native-memory qualification requires a selected update > 0; at least
five percentage points of semantic gain over its initialization and its own
erased-history intervention; physical MSE at most 75% of erased-history MSE;
finite results; source/split integrity; exact replay and persistent delivery.
Compare native versus delayed descriptively with paired, premise-group bootstrap
intervals (2,000 replicates, seed 191190); one training seed does not estimate
training-seed variability. Preserve an unqualified candidate for diagnosis.
Do not replace the earlier qualified owner based on unequal curricula.

Independent checked progress is recorded as a signed improvement tied to the
original task, exact predictor, evidence and decision; duplicate or stale credit
is rejected. Training labels supervise parameter updates. A signed score alone
is not described as a learned reward policy. Delivery demonstrates a saved
observation history, several conditional questions, an actual additional
measurement, independently measured answer change and a returned original goal.
