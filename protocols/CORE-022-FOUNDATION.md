# CORE-022: fresh coupled-owner foundation course

This is the first teaching phase of the complete-core work order, after build
acceptance. It does not complete the broader research or qualify later behavior
from construction tests. The further persistent investigation, representation,
procedure-learning and five-use correction assessments remain explicit work.

## Fixed owner and teaching

Initialize every trainable weight from seed **22271**, with width 48, four
boundary nodes, two split-integration rounds, three conditional branches and
eight executable instruction slots. Load no old checkpoint or pretrained tensor.
The same reciprocal energy, covariance, fast/slow memory, active bulk, flow,
finite neural action and fusion machinery is active from the first input.
Canonical halves use echo credit; irreversible operations retain their stated
derivatives. Independent checks, observed source records and conditional states
remain distinct. Episodic situations reset between unrelated teaching examples;
the common learned weights and optimizer persist across all subjects.

Train two same-initialization owners for **12,288 updates each**: `credited` and
`withheld`. They receive the same source order, examples, performed controls,
initial random states, AdamW (learning rate 0.001, weight decay 0.0001), norm clip
1 and update count. The primary `credited` owner also receives the independently
measured mixed-investigation policy credit. The control withholds that credit;
both retain the same entropy regularizer and supervised outcome learning. This
comparison tests a contribution; conventional architecture superiority is not
the research goal.

The twelve-update cycle is semantic, physics, executable programs, human pairs,
reading, semantic, physics, executable programs, human pairs, mixed investigation,
semantic, human pairs. Batch size is eight except reading and mixed investigation,
which use four. Thus each arm receives **90,112 lesson presentations**, including
24,576 semantic, 16,384 physical, 16,384 executable, 24,576 human-pair, 4,096 reading
and 4,096 mixed episodes. These counts are presentations, not distinct records.
Pair subjects rotate evenly across available dictionary, dialogue, grammar,
philosophy, calculus and programming sources. All individual identities and
actual teaching costs are logged.

Human targets remain supplied human annotations. Program teaching marginalizes
compatible source-derived graphs and latent branches; use loss weight 0.1 to
account for several structural decisions per program. Other track weights are 1.
The human code subset and instance-scoped arithmetic contracts are reported by
their actual counts. Human reading or source-pair choice is not relabeled
open-ended code generation or complete literary interpretation.

The simulation teacher is the stated linear/quadratic drag, mass and offset
family. Independently written scalar outcome evaluation provides policy credit.
The mixed episodes counterbalance observation order and family independently.
Teacher mechanism parameters never enter learner input. Uniform behavior chooses
one of nine finite controls or STOP, with probability 0.1, identically between
arms. Original-goal checked error reduction minus 0.001 observation cost supplies
credit, clipped at +/-0.25 and scaled by four in the existing importance-weighted
policy objective. No reward is supplied for confidence or branch count.

## Sources and untouched assessments

Use `local/CORE-022-course-v1/MANIFEST.json` and its fixed source-group allocation.
Hash buckets reserve separate development and final groups across every view;
the same original group cannot cross tracks into teaching. Earlier opened finals
are not reused. Source transformations were developed before this allocation;
no candidate predictions or losses determined the allocation. Final records are
opened only by a separately identified assessment invocation after teaching.

Development selects up to 64 records per track using the fixed allocation key,
plus 64 new physical systems and 32 mixed episodes. Assess initial weights and
every 1,024 updates. The selection score is the unweighted mean of six reported
quantities: semantic accuracy, human-pair accuracy macro-averaged over available
subjects, reading accuracy, independently executed program-instance accuracy,
1/(1+physical MSE), and the mean of mixed semantic accuracy and
1/(1+returned-goal MSE). This is a selection rule, not an intelligence scale.
Select the largest score, with earlier updates winning exact ties. Retain every
candidate, failure, development result and cost; no tuning on final results.

The reserved source pool has 10,723 groups. The later whole-owner assessment must
also measure five uses of the same acquired relationship, coherent correction,
retention, transfer, conditional-state purity, distinct checked methods and exact
restart. A passed foundation score alone does not close those obligations.

## Persistence and resource contract

One exclusive numerical lease, one CPU thread and a 2 GiB process-tree cap; no
paid service. Save content-addressed owner, optimizer, random states, cursor,
teaching counts and development history every 64 updates and at development
selection. Flush and fsync teaching receipts before advancing the saved cursor.
Preserve any unsaved failed tail and its cost; record replay from the durable
cursor explicitly. A resume rejects changed teaching code, protocol or source
identities. After completion, the selected primary remains the same learner for
the subsequent integrated assessments and incremental learning; no fresh
initialization substitutes for its continuation.

Before launch require full current regression, source-view checks, course resource
workload, exact checkpoint continuation and the architecture/source acceptance
record. Do not launch this course by copying an engineering fixture's weights.
