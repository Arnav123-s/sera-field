# CONCEPT-009: repair the identified language-routing bottleneck

CONCEPT-008 completed two 4,096-step acquisition studies and its 2,048-episode
reward study. Its development evidence already identified 75% routing accuracy
on the four paraphrases. The supported final connected-direction score was
69.53%, while the numerical acquisition/transfer measures improved. Preserve
that failure; do not relabel the earlier owner as fully qualified.

The repair changes only question_route parameters of the development-selected
CONCEPT-008 owner, including its qualified reward policy if that policy passes
the frozen reward decision. Every numerical, meaning, reading and mathematics
parameter remains bit-identical. Use paired question contexts that differ in
the named variable to discourage reliance on incidental sentence form.

This is a supplied, bounded two-variable simulation-language teaching set. It is
not an additional human corpus and is not counted as autonomous linguistic
discovery. The full natural-language goal remains the broader acceptance target.

## Finite protocol

Freeze the generated train, development and final phrase banks before training.
Require disjoint exact normalized questions. Train the existing head for 1,536
batches of 32 paired questions, seed 9109, AdamW lr .001, weight decay .0001.
The word encoder remains the original human-trained encoder. Cache its frozen
representations, add independent normal input noise SD .03 during teaching,
and use cross-entropy plus .1 times a paired correct-direction margin loss.
Cache validation checks compare live encoded outputs before use.

Assess the complete development phrase bank at zero and every 128 updates.
Select highest routing accuracy, then lowest cross-entropy, then earliest step.
Retain all updates and optimizer/RNG state. The final phrase bank is not inspected
for optimization. No new physics or investigation-policy training in this repair.

Register selected weights before assessment. Re-evaluate all five views on new
CONCEPT-009 simulated systems (512 supported, 128 omitted), using the independently
written observation teacher and DOP853 checker. Compare the repaired owner, its
unchanged language parent and the original ECHO-007 owner on the same new systems.
Use the reserved new final phrase bank. Do not reuse CONCEPT-008 final cases.

Require >=90% final routing, >=80% correct grounded direction including routing,
at least 10 percentage points better direction than the unchanged language parent,
and identical numerical outputs to that parent. Retain the original numerical
qualification gates versus ECHO-007: .8 of parent MSE in prediction, inverse and
counterfactual; .9 in planning. The field, acquired relationship and retained
human abilities must stay intact. Verify persistent session creation, evidence
append, returned answers and exact restart.

Fresh-process replay verifies every raw case and metric. Any remaining failure
is retained with its measured scope; never tune on this final cohort.
One CPU numerical thread, the shared exclusive lease, a 2 GiB process-tree cap,
and no paid compute. Count this repair and the failed earlier qualification.
