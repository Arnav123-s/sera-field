independent symmetric signs define z = sum_i s_i F_i(c) + sigma epsilon, with

K(J|c) = sigma^2 J.J/2 + sum_i log cosh(J.F_i(c)).

is -2 sum_i F_i tensor F_i tensor F_i tensor F_i. The conditional fourth-order
Edgeworth action and its approximation error are explicitly evaluated; the exact
