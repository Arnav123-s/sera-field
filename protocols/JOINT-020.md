# JOINT-020: checked investigation and learning through one retained situation

Prospective finite course, recorded before numerical execution. NATIVE-019's
three completed curricula, finals and failed replay attempt stay immutable.
This work continues the primary native owner; it does not select an architecture
from NATIVE-019's opened final results.

## Question and connection to the research

Can the same from-scratch-trained field retain a human-described situation and
physical observations, imagine a useful intervention, use independently checked
progress to update its existing decision weights, and return to both questions?
No second answer model, policy head or private memory bank is introduced. The
existing choice map scores a supplied finite observation grid and STOP. Human
and physical episodes are paired as an engineered interference test: the human
prose is not falsely presented as a description of the simulated apparatus.

This closes a concrete integration gap between NATIVE-019's jointly taught
memory and its fixed investigation selector. It does not rename numerical
memory, three branch offsets or a supplied action grid as autonomous concept
creation, infinitely many futures or the full proposed research substrate.

## Sources and partitions

Use the same immutable human training partitions and verified source manifests
as NATIVE-019. Human relation annotations are supplied by MultiNLI. The other
human reading, arithmetic and source-passage tracks are rehearsed unchanged.
New training order and physical namespaces begin `JOINT-020-`. The physical
families remain linear/quadratic drag, varying mass and force offset; these
families, variables and exact simulator are engineering, not discovered concepts.

Human development uses 128 rows deterministically ranked from the existing
development partition. Each of two final cohorts selects the first 128 complete
premise groups under the new deterministic rank, after excluding every complete
group touched by GENRE-017 or NATIVE-019 finals. Register source IDs and hashes
before training. There is no final-based selection or repair tuning.

## Finite curriculum and training methods

Start both `credited` and `withheld` arms from NATIVE-019/native's development
selection (update 8,192), with identical fresh AdamW state. Train 1,024 cycles of
16 situations per arm. Each cycle is followed by one 16-item rehearsal update
from NATIVE's eight-step course. Each arm therefore receives 16,384 joint
episodes and 16,384 rehearsal presentations, with 2,048 optimizer updates.
The same deterministic ordering and uniformly explored actions are supplied to
both arms. This keeps physical measurement access identical during teaching.

Each mixed situation contains the entire human premise and two physical
measurements, in one of two alternating chronological orders. Ask the original
human relation and physical goal; select one of nine force/velocity pairs in
{-1,0,1} squared or STOP; obtain the actual simulated measurement; ask the
original questions again; compute independent signed physical-error reduction;
record positive/negative traces; ask a different physical query. The new query's
answer is excluded from observations. STOP adds no observation and has zero
progress and zero measurement cost.

Both arms receive the same supervised human and numerical feedback. The
credited arm additionally updates the existing choice map and its shared
antecedents using a score-function policy loss, corrected by the recorded
uniform behavior probability. Credit is clipped to [-.25,.25] and scaled by 4
for that objective only. The raw unrounded progress remains in the record.
Measurement cost is .001. Both arms receive the same .002 entropy coefficient;
only the independently checked reward term is removed in `withheld`.

The combined objective is .5 times the sum of before/after human cross-entropies,
plus .5 times the sum of before/after physical MSE, plus the next-query MSE,
plus the policy objective. AdamW rate .0001, decay .0001, gradient norm cap 1.
All existing native parameters may learn. No encoder, memory or answer head is
silently frozen. Credit marks can affect later queries but cannot write an
invented measurement. The requested/performed action check gates policy credit.

Select every 128 cycles using 128 development episodes, by mean of human
accuracy after investigation and 1/(1+physical goal MSE after investigation).
Also test initial state. Earliest tie wins. Log every source ID, action, behavior
probability, objective component, gradient norm and reward; save optimizer, RNG,
exact next cycle and source identities every 128 cycles. No repeated final use.

## Independent assessment and whole-cycle checks

Finish both courses before opening final predictions. Each final cohort runs
the same owner through language, observations, proposal, actual measurement,
checked progress, next query and returned answer. Final weights are fixed;
original-goal error is measured before its grading signal reaches the state.
The later-query assessment explicitly includes that prior graded feedback.

Compare credited, withheld, initial, and the credited predictor with uniform,
fixed branch-disagreement or STOP investigation. Test inference removal of
memory and query settling separately. These removals are diagnostic
interventions, not separately trained architectures. A new shifted-control
cohort (256 simulations) checks longer histories and controls outside training
range. Retention includes the reserved human cohorts in ordinary native mode
and 256 fresh four-observation physical episodes, plus the existing development
tracks labeled as development. Group-bootstrap comparisons use 2,000 resamples,
seed 202020, with fixed-model sampling scope stated.

Qualification requires all engineering/source/replay gates, changed learned
decision weights, improved mixed-task goal MSE relative to the initial owner,
human retention within three percentage points, physical retention within 10%,
and positive mean credited-versus-withheld investigation utility with a 95%
paired group interval above zero. If a gate fails, preserve results and diagnose;
do not call a reward counter or an optimizer step a learned improvement.

A persistent delivery must bind decision, original goals, predictor, before and
after state, evidence and independent verifier; reject repeated/stale credit;
check actual versus requested controls; save a real reward-driven weight update;
re-encode retained observations under the changed predictor; and restore the
exact owner, optimizer and original goals in a fresh process. The returned
answer and next-query test must remain separate from grading on the same answer.

## Costs, competing methods and scope

One CPU numerical thread, exclusive existing lease, 2 GiB process-tree cap,
no paid services. Full failed and successful attempts count. Fresh replay runs
after the training interpreter exits to avoid overlapping retained allocations.
The primary distinction is whether credit improves the learned procedure, not
whether this architecture wins against every classical method.

[Policy-gradient theory](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/1999/hash/464d828b85b0bed98e80ade0a5c43b0f-Abstract.html)
supports a learned decision rule; the fixed uniform and disagreement selectors
remain informative controls. This finite contextual investigation is an
engineering approximation of the broader requested curiosity, not evidence
that supplied tasks, variables or bounds were chosen independently by SERA.
