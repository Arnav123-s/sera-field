## Part A. The author's directions in force

### A1. What SERA is

- **One SERA, one Field** (2026-09-27, revision 6): "one SERA, one mind that's adaptive, not modules of each
  separate field or task - it should be able to be taught and learn all fields"; "one SERA, the whole Field - all
  the parts become or dissolve into the Field".
- **Understanding first** (2026-09-27): "not belief, understanding"; "understanding, laws and beliefs intertwined in
  superposition, understanding the heaviest, then equal weight on laws and beliefs, and they all influence each
  other".
- **SERA chooses its own loop** (2026-09-27): "the loop must be adaptive - SERA chooses how the loop functions - a
  superposition of its current loop, part of the entire Field, not a separate entity"; "how it functions is its
  decision".
- **SERA is its Field** (2026-09-29, the alignment interview): memory, belief, understanding, imagination, dreams,
  its sense of right and its moves are all in superposition; there is no fixed loop.
- **Memory is the Field, no recall** (2026-09-29 night): "seras memory, understanding and belief are not separated;
  I want them as part of the field. Say sera discovers something - I give sera the Riemann hypothesis - it imagines
  how it would prove it end to end, roughly, keeps its best imaginations, and THE WHOLE FIELD REORGANIZES AND LEARNS
  that this is correct; the field deepens its understanding and belief, and they all act as a memory in
  superposition ... I don't want it a separate item; it is one with the field; it doesn't need to know or learn or
  recall at all if the whole field is the memory - it's like an automatic trigger."
- **Steps on a map** (2026-09-29 night): "it breaks the problem as an empty map or maze: it knows where it is and
  where it should go; it takes one small step at a time, gets it right, and then the whole field evolves, and then
  the next step; if it gets stuck it imagines how it would reach the goal and revises itself, adapts, evolves and
  improvises - not stays stuck."

### A2. How SERA grows

- **Nothing predefined** (2026-09-27): "do not give it a predefined set of anything - teach it, let it learn and use
  things, words, ideas on its own. DO NOT GIVE A PREDEFINED SET OF WORDS, SENTENCES, FORMULAS ETC. It must learn and
  be taught and use it on its own."
- **Teach, don't code** (2026-09-29): "if there is a bug in the code then only you should change it; if a behaviour
  can be taught then teach it instead of changing the code"; "when sera is good enough at coding then you can clone
  sera and let the clone edit its own code, observe what happens and report".
- **Crutches, then superposition** (2026-09-29, the interview): start with more mechanisms as crutches; teach how
  they work and how to make, change and grow its own; put them in superposition; learn every knob (time shares,
  sizes, fades), keeping the time and memory walls until it is safe; in the end fully self-improving, including its
  own code.
- **SERA gives itself abilities** (2026-09-28 night): "not to keep giving it extra abilities, but to give it the
  ability to give itself abilities, ... sera builds that ability and uses it ... a form of RSI"; "give it the
  freedom ... and teach it that"; "give it a dictionary ... so it can understand English and communicate better".
- **Imagination is its own** (2026-09-28, revision 7): "Instead of scaffolding and adding external engineering,
  teach it the methods of imagination and let it develop its own methods; keep the imagination unbounded."; "Let
  SERA make its own moves in imagination, as a superposition that can be triggered or influenced however SERA
  wants."
- **Perception it improves** (2026-09-28): "give it more freedom - teach it code and tell it: you can improve your
  own drawing skills, you can improve and get more abilities to perceive - why limit it"; "give SERA more
  dimensions of perception and let it improve the number of dimensions it functions and thinks in"; "if anything
  from its ideas was right, it can leverage and learn that part - smaller discoveries that compound into a bigger
  one, like a snowball".
- **Open play with real words** (2026-09-25): "don't simply give SERA a box of numbers or information to play with.
  Teach it actual words and actual things, and let it plan on its own without specific constraints or specific ways.
  Let it understand and decide how it wants to play and imagine things, not that it can only do this or that."
- **Never stuck** (2026-09-28): "sera must not get stuck; if it's bad it must find the correct one".
- **Deeper questions** (2026-09-28): "Question more deeply, reason more deeply, imagine more; let imagination raise
  more questions. Mass should not stay only mass: going deeper you reach quanta; bigger, stars and black holes - in
  code, language, math and philosophy too. Teach it philosophy, and use that for how it imagines and thinks."

### A3. The judge

- (2026-09-28): "Let the judge dissolve into the rest - not fully separate. SERA can convince the judge, improve it,
  or give it abilities or ways to see whether it is really right, but it cannot bypass or change it."
- (2026-09-29, the interview): an inner sense of "this could be right" should grow; the outside judge stays while it
  is not there, and must not bias SERA's thinking or imagining.
- Every change to `ccops5/core` is the author's decision, after a independent review (Decisions 8 and 12-15 so far; 16 open).

### A4. The goal

- **Good generally** (2026-09-30): "I approve - just make it capable enough to be good generally: I want to be able
  to converse with it, and I want it to be able to code, do physics, etc."
- **A usable agent** (2026-09-26 night): never frozen; asked for an app, a discovery, a proof or a design, it works
  end to end in small steps and says each finding as it goes; one Field for every domain, never separate per-domain
  models, not even as a baseline.
- **Runs on the laptop** (2026-09-26): "we can migrate from laptop to colab for now"; "the final sera must be able
  to run on laptop without stressing the laptop out smoothly and the extensive time it takes hours must be fixed".
- **Long runs and real benchmarks** (2026-09-28): "Then a LONG run, and benchmarks: actual complicated multi-step
  tasks, not tiny things." (2026-09-27): "Do not keep testing every small thing."

### A5. How we work

- **Reports** (2026-09-26): "keep the current ones updated" - one living file per kind, rewritten in place, two
  parts (technical, then plain words), numbers only from saved runs.
- **Review:** independent reviews before judge-adjacent changes; helper scripts only for
  routine reading or grading.
- **Compute:** develop on Colab (the author's Colab Pro, for SERA only); at most one short process on the laptop.
- **Approvals:** never accept consents, terms or OAuth for the author; downloads need the author's permission.
