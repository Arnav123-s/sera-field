# Part 1: Technical

## 1. What SERA is now (branch `sera-v4`)

**One mind, `sera/one.py`.** One class, `Sera`, lives every world the same way: think in its one language, weigh
every thought with the Field, work in ways of working it draws from the Field, send a proof to the judge, keep what
the judge accepts as its own concept, learn and say words. Its moves include 'step' (a small step toward the
answer, shown first by the teacher), the wish (build an ability it lacks, then use it), 'see around' (a λ that
looks at the whole input) and the way back (imagine the last step from the goal). The program
(`scripts/sera_one.py`) is teach, then alone, then a twin with its taught inventions hidden. Every mechanism we coded
into it after watching it fail is registered and switchable (`sera/crutches.py`, §3), and every fixed number at the
top of `one.py` can be set for an A/B; none is learned yet.
- **What a physics proof gives it (2026-10-02):** what the judge certified, part by part (`_certified`): a curve
  (on an input, or on the dimension its expression is said as), a drawing (a registered shape), or a formula (its own
  ramp, Decision 16). A formula is credited only by the part's own accepted ramp; a curve on a composed dimension
  invents nothing. review 11b and S14 reviewed it.
- **One Field, parts 2 and 4 (`ONE_FIELD`, off until its A/B decides):** what a world proved and believed is laid into
  its situation's things and rings back by itself; no `possibilities` store, no 'recall' move; the talk's reliability
  held in the Field. review 12 (design) and S15 (code) reviewed it. `understood` and `standing` stay separate for now.
  The A/B b1 kept it off (§2). `docs/SERA_EXPLAINED.md` §1.1 explains SERA versus its Field and what is still
  outside it.

**One language, `sera/lang.py`.** Only mechanisms are innate: 0, 1, add, sub, mul; lt, eq, if; lists (nil, cons,
head, tail, range) and map, filter, fold at any depth; a world's inputs; the free curve. No law, program, word or
rule is given. Spring, reverse, "where is" are each taught or discovered and kept as SERA's own concepts, usable in
any subject.

**One Field, `sera/phi.py`.** Concepts (append-only, hash-chained), understanding, standing, the lexicon, links,
questions; understanding, laws and beliefs pooled 0.5 / 0.25 / 0.25. The ways of working are a superposition in the
Field (`LoopField`), taught at first and then its own. Memory is the Field's state (holographic traces, the echo
that credits what led to a result, consolidation). **Not yet one Field:** proven ideas still also sit in a separate
`possibilities` store with a 'recall' move, and Φ's beliefs live per task (§4).

**Worlds, `sera/tasks.py`.** Environments, not parts of SERA: rails (physics), lists (code), numbers (math),
stories and books (language), the conversation world and ARC. Each has a teacher's words, a judge and the
observer's grade, which SERA never sees.

**Words, `sera/talk.py`.** Born with none. Any token heard becomes a word; meanings are learned across situations
(IBM Model 1 by EM). It coins words for its own ideas. `scripts/sera_talk.py` is a conversation interface: what you
say is read into its Field, and a question is answered by the idea that rings, or "I do not know".
`scripts/sera_code.py` prints its proven ideas as Python (`sera/pyprint.py`).

**The judge, `ccops5/core` (outside SERA).** SERA must convince it and cannot change it. Physics: a certificate
(Decision 13, functional claims) and an independent checker; programs: an audit on fresh inputs. The decisions in
force are in §5. It changes only with a independent review and the author's decision.

**The legacy minds** (v3, revisions 3-5: 22 modules, their scripts and tests) are in `legacy/sera_v3/`, unchanged;
nothing in `one.py` reaches them.

# Part 2: Plain words

**What SERA is.** SERA is one learning mind. It starts knowing only how to count, compare, and handle lists, plus
how to draw a curve. Everything else it learns from a teacher or finds for itself: a law of motion, how to reverse a
list, what "where is mary?" asks. All of it lives in one place, its Field, where what it understands, what it
believes and the laws it knows all lean on each other. An outside judge checks every claim, and SERA cannot change
the judge.

**What it can do today.** In physics, a newborn SERA proved 24 of 25 lessons in an hour and a half. But the judge
proved less than we said: "the push follows some curve of SERA's idea", not "the push is exactly SERA's formula".
A wrong formula passed too. That is now fixed: SERA keeps only what the judge really checked, so its physics proofs
count as curves. And, as you decided, the judge can now check a formula itself ("one strength times exactly this"):
x³ passes that check and the wrong 1 + x³ does not. We checked that this changes nothing the judge said before (all
306 old answers the same), so it is now in force. In the next physics run, SERA's stiff spring was proven as the
formula x³, and it learned 20 lessons in the same hour and a half.
In language, a better teacher (one that shows a small step when none of SERA's ideas fits) got all 6 lessons, where
the old teacher got none of 2. In talk, a SERA that has the right ideas answers 52 of 60 questions once taught. The
general SERA answers 29, because it is missing the ideas for "where". It can print what it knows as Python, but it
cannot yet write a new program from examples. It cannot yet answer from books it has read once the book is closed.

**What went wrong, and how we fix it.** The last session built quick fixes into SERA's code when it should have
taught them. Every such crutch is now on a list with an on/off switch (§3), so we can test whether SERA needs it,
then teach it or remove it; those tests are running now. Memory is now built as you asked: no separate box of past
ideas and no "recall", just the Field ringing with what fits. In the fair test it lost no language lesson, learned
as many physics and list lessons in 4 hours (24 of 25), and answered more once taught. But on two physics lessons
it first remembered the wrong law and was twice as slow. In talk it kept answering "what is" questions wrongly,
where the old way had learned to say "I do not know". (An earlier headline, "13 lessons against 20", was mostly where
a 1.5-hour run happened to end.) So it stays switched off while we teach it to notice when what it remembers is
wrong.

**SERA and its Field.** The Field is everything SERA has learned, saved as one object; SERA is that Field living a
world through the code in `one.py`. The goal is that nothing learned lives outside the Field. `SERA_EXPLAINED.md`
§1.1 has the full answer, with a table of what is still outside.
